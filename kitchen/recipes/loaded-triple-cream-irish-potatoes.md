# Loaded Triple Cream Irish Potatoes

- Source: Pepper Belly Pete (TikTok / Facebook). Text below came from a
  web-search summary the user pasted, not a verbatim transcript, so amounts may
  be approximate.
- Method: stovetop + oven casserole
- Type: creamy mashed-potato side (cream cheese, sour cream, heavy cream,
  parmesan, bacon, cheddar)

## Ingredients
- Potatoes: 4 lbs peeled and chopped potatoes (or 6 russet potatoes)
- Butter: 1 stick of butter
- Cream Cheese: 8 oz block of cream cheese
- Sour Cream: 8 oz container of sour cream
- Heavy Cream: 1 pint (16 oz) heavy cream
- Cheese: 3 oz grated Parmesan cheese (plus 8 oz shredded cheddar cheese for topping)
- Bacon: 10 slices of cooked and crumbled bacon
- Seasonings: 1 teaspoon garlic powder, 1 teaspoon ground black pepper, 1 teaspoon salt or Creole seasoning
- Garnish: Optional green onions (chopped)

## Instructions
1. Boil the Potatoes:
   - Place the peeled and chopped potatoes in a large pot and cover them with water.
   - Add a generous amount of salt and bring to a boil until the potatoes are fork-tender.
   - Drain the water completely, then return the potatoes to the warm pot for a moment to dry out slightly.
2. Make the Cream Sauce:
   - In a large pan or skillet over medium-low heat, combine the stick of butter, cream cheese, sour cream, heavy cream, grated Parmesan cheese, garlic powder, black pepper, and salt or Creole seasoning.
   - Stir continuously until the mixture is smooth and completely incorporated.
   - Fold in the cooked and crumbled bacon.
3. Mash and Combine:
   - Add the drained, tender potatoes into the cream sauce.
   - Use a potato masher or an electric mixer to blend the mixture until smooth and creamy.
4. Bake (Loaded Version):
   - Transfer the potato mixture into a greased, oven-safe casserole dish.
   - Top with 8 oz of freshly shredded cheddar cheese.
   - Bake at 300°F for about 30 minutes until the cheese is melted and golden, then garnish with green onions if desired.
