# Bottom of the Barrel Chili

- Source: shared by the user (original author unknown)
- Serves: about 12 bowls (can halve, or make it all and freeze half)
- Cook: crock pot, low all day or high 2 hours

## Ingredients
- 2 lbs. round steak (freeze meat for 45 min before dicing; you want small tiny cubes)
- 2 lbs chuck steak (sear meat in 1 cup of veg oil, then drain)
  - (if you want you can use 4 lbs of ground beef Angus, it's easy, no cutting up beef)
  - Note: if you get real lean beef, won't taste as good

In a crock pot add ingredients to meat and keep lid on while cooking:
- 2 cans of favorite beer (won't make you drunk, it cooks out)
- 2 cups water
- 1/2 c dried chili peppers (hard to find, this is optional)
- 2 cans of Diced Rotel tomatoes
- 2 sm cans of tomato paste (add 2nd can later if too runny, it will thicken up)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 1/2 tsp oregano
- 1 tsp paprika
- 2 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 finely chopped onion (I like to sauté them w/garlic before I put them in, your choice). Use a sweet onion
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper
- 4 tbsp chili powder (a couple more may be added if you want hotter chili, which can be added later)
- 1 tbsp peanut butter

On the side:
- 2 cans of your favorite chili beans. Cook chili beans separate from chili
- Sharp cheddar cheese, grated, and diced onions for guests to add

## Directions
1. Freeze the round steak 45 minutes, then dice into small tiny cubes.
2. Sear the meat in 1 cup of veg oil, then drain. (Or use 4 lbs ground beef.)
3. In a crock pot, add all the ingredients to the meat. Keep the lid on while cooking.
4. Stir well. Cook on low for the day, or on high for 2 hours, stirring occasionally.
5. Cook the chili beans separately from the chili.
6. Serve with grated sharp cheddar and diced onions for guests to add.

## Notes
- If the chili is too runny, add the second can of tomato paste; it will thicken.
- Lean beef won't taste as good.
- Makes about 12 bowls. Can cut in half, or make the full batch and freeze half.
