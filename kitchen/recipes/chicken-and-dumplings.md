# Chicken and Dumplings (homemade)

- Source: NOT a specific website. The web search only returned a general summary
  (pages blocked by the network policy), so this is a standard version written
  from the common method those recipes share. Quantities are typical, not
  copied from a source. Replace with the user's preferred recipe if they share one.
- Method: Dutch oven or large pot, stovetop
- Serves: about 6
- Time: about 1 hour

## Ingredients
Soup
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 3 carrots, sliced
- 3 stalks celery, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
- 8 cups chicken broth (low-sodium)
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme (or a few fresh sprigs)
- 2 bay leaves
- 3 to 4 cups shredded cooked chicken (about 1 rotisserie chicken)
- 1/2 cup milk or cream (optional)
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Chopped fresh parsley, to finish

Dumplings
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 4 tablespoons cold butter, cut small
- 3/4 to 1 cup milk

## Instructions
1. Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion, carrots and celery with a pinch of salt and pepper and cook until tender, about 5 to 6 minutes. Add the garlic for the last minute.
2. Sprinkle in the flour and stir for 1 to 2 minutes. Slowly whisk in the chicken broth, then add the thyme and bay leaves. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer for about 10 minutes to thicken slightly.
3. Stir in the shredded chicken (and the milk or cream, if using). Taste and adjust the salt and pepper. Remove the bay leaves.
4. Make the dumplings: whisk the flour, baking powder and salt. Cut in the cold butter with a fork or pastry blender until crumbly, then stir in the milk until a soft dough forms. Don't overmix.
5. Bring the soup back to a gentle boil. Drop the dough in spoonfuls (about 2 tablespoons each) onto the surface. Cover and simmer, without lifting the lid, for 15 to 20 minutes, until the dumplings are puffed and cooked through.
6. Sprinkle with parsley and serve hot.

## Notes
- Starting from raw chicken instead of rotisserie: simmer 1 1/2 lb chicken breasts
  or thighs in the broth for about 20 minutes, then shred and return to the pot.
- Keep the simmer gentle once the dumplings are in; a hard boil makes them fall apart.
