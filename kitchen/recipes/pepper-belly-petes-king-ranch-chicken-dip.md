# Pepper Belly Pete's King Ranch Chicken Dip

- Source: Pepper Belly Pete (YouTube Short / TikTok). Text below came from a
  web-search summary the user pasted, not a verbatim transcript, so amounts
  (especially the seasoning) may be approximate. Check against the video if exact
  quantities matter.
- Method: slow cooker (crock pot)
- Type: rich queso-style dip with chicken, vegetables and melted cheese

## Ingredients
- 1 medium onion (diced)
- 8 oz fresh baby bella mushrooms (diced)
- 2 jalapeños (diced)
- 4 oz Hatch green chilies
- 10.5 oz can cream of chicken with herbs soup
- 10 oz can Ro-Tel tomatoes and green chilies
- 1 fully cooked, deboned chicken (shredded or chopped)
- 16 oz pepper jack cheese (shredded)
- 16 oz American cheese (diced or from the deli counter)
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 oz packet ranch dressing mix
- Zippy Zap (or Pepper Belly Pete's preferred spice/zippy seasoning blend, about 2 tablespoons)

## Instructions
1. Combine the base: Add the diced onion, baby bella mushrooms, jalapeños, Hatch green chilies, cream of chicken with herbs, and Ro-Tel into a slow cooker (crockpot).
2. Add the protein and dairy: Mix in the cooked and deboned chicken, pepper jack cheese, American cheese, chicken broth, heavy cream, ranch dressing mix, and your zippy seasoning.
3. Cook on high: Cover with the lid and cook on high for the first 30 minutes.
4. Stir thoroughly: Open the lid and stir the mixture well.
5. Simmer on low: Reduce the slow cooker heat to low and continue stirring every 30 minutes until all the cheeses are completely melted and the ingredients are fully incorporated.
