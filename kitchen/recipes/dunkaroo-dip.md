# Dunkaroo Dip

- Source: Ashley (recipe blog post)
- Recipe type: Dessert / Cuisine: American
- Prep: 5 mins / Total: 5 mins (plus at least 2 hours chilling)
- Serves: 8-10
- Description: the easiest dessert dip there is, perfect for a party; tastes
  just like the beloved Dunkaroos snack

## Ingredients
- 1 box Funfetti cake mix
- 1⅓ cup vanilla yogurt
- 2 cups Cool Whip
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Sprinkles, for garnish

## Instructions
1. Mix all of the ingredients (except for the extra sprinkles) together in a bowl until completely combined and no lumps remain.
2. Chill in refrigerator for at least two hours (dip will be too soft without doing this step).
3. Garnish with extra sprinkles for a pretty presentation.
4. Serve with animal crackers, teddy grahams, pretzels, graham crackers, apples or Nilla Wafers. Using a variety of dippers would be the best option. Enjoy!

## Notes
- The cake mix is used dry (uncooked). Raw flour can carry bacteria, so some
  cooks heat-treat the mix first (spread on a tray, bake to 165°F, cool).
