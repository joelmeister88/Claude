# French Onion Soup (classic)

- Source: web search summary of classic recipes (e.g. Sur La Table's Classic
  French Onion Soup). The full pages were blocked by the network policy, so only
  the quantities returned by the search are listed. Amounts marked "not given"
  were missing. Replace with the exact page text when it can be fetched.
- Method: Dutch oven on the stovetop, finish under the broiler
- Time: roughly 2 to 2½ hours, mostly caramelizing the onions

## Ingredients
- 4 tablespoons butter
- 8 cups thinly sliced yellow onion
- ½ teaspoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- ¾ teaspoon freshly ground pepper
- 5 fresh thyme sprigs
- 1 tablespoon flour
- 8 cups unsalted beef stock
- 1 cup dry white wine
- Baguette slices (amount not given; 2 slices per bowl)
- Garlic, for rubbing the bread (amount not given; 1 clove)
- 4 cups shredded Gruyère cheese

## Instructions
1. Caramelize the onions: Melt the butter in a large Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the onion and sugar, partially cover, and cook, stirring occasionally, until the onion is very soft, about 20 minutes.
2. Add the salt, pepper, and thyme. Reduce the heat to medium-low and cook uncovered, stirring occasionally, until the onions are deep brown and caramelized, about 1 hour.
3. Discard the thyme sprigs. Add the flour and cook, stirring, for 1 minute. Slowly add the hot stock, then the wine. Increase the heat to high and boil for 5 minutes, then reduce the heat to low and cook uncovered for 40 minutes.
4. Heat the broiler. Rub one side of each baguette slice with garlic. Ladle the soup into oven-safe bowls, top each with 2 slices of baguette, and divide the cheese among the bowls, covering the bread. Broil until the cheese melts and bubbles, about 4 to 5 minutes.
