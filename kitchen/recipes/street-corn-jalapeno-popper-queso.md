# Street Corn Jalapeño Popper Queso

- Source: "Dip Me Daddy" (weekly football-season dip series, social media post)
- Method: crock pot
- Pairs with: Doritos or tortilla chips

## Ingredients
- 16 oz cream cheese
- 16 oz sour cream
- 1/2 block of Velveeta (or the whole thing)
- 1/2 cup chopped cilantro
- 1 cup pickled jalapeño (or fresh)
- 2 cups chopped bacon
- 1 tbsp Tajin
- Juice of 2 limes
- 1 can Hatch Rotel
- 1 can street corn

## Directions
1. Throw your ingredients into the crock pot. (Cut up the Velveeta, dice the cilantro, drain the corn.)
2. Turn it on high.
3. Cover and let it sit for 45 minutes.
4. Stir it up WELL.
5. Cover for another 15-30 minutes and enjoy.

## Notes
- Pairs amazing with Doritos or tortilla chips.
