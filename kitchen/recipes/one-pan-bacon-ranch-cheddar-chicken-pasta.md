# One Pan Bacon Ranch Cheddar Chicken Pasta

- Source: shared by the user
- Method: one large skillet

## Ingredients
- 12 ounces bacon, chopped
- 1 small yellow onion, diced
- 1 ½ pounds chicken breast, cut into bite-sized cubes
- 1 to 1 ½ tablespoons ranch seasoning
- 1 to 1 ½ teaspoons Slap Ya Mama seasoning (optional, for spice)
- 2 tablespoons cornstarch
- 4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 pound pasta of choice (Radiatori works especially well)
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 2 to 3 cups freshly shredded cheddar cheese

## Instructions
1. In a large skillet, cook the chopped bacon over medium-low heat until about ¾ cooked. Add the diced onion and continue cooking until the bacon is crispy and the onion is tender. Remove the bacon and onion mixture from the skillet, leaving the bacon grease in the pan.
2. In a large bowl, combine the chicken, ranch seasoning, Slap Ya Mama seasoning, and cornstarch. Toss until the chicken is evenly coated.
3. Increase the skillet heat to medium-high and add the seasoned chicken to the bacon grease. Cook for 3 minutes, flip the chicken, and cook for another 3 minutes. Stir for about 30 seconds more until lightly browned and cooked through. Remove the chicken from the skillet.
4. Pour the chicken broth into the skillet and bring to a boil. Add the pasta and season lightly again with ranch seasoning and Slap Ya Mama seasoning, if desired. Cook for 8–10 minutes, stirring every couple of minutes, until the pasta is tender and most of the liquid has been absorbed.
5. Reduce the heat to low and stir in the heavy cream. Add the shredded cheddar cheese and stir until melted and creamy.
6. Return the cooked bacon and chicken to the skillet. Stir everything together until well combined and heated through.
7. Serve warm and enjoy!
