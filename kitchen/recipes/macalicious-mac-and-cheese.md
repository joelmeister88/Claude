# Pepper Belly Pete's "Macalicious" Mac and Cheese

- Source: Pepper Belly Pete (TikTok / Facebook / recipe PDF). Text below came
  from a web-search summary the user pasted, not a verbatim transcript, so
  amounts may be approximate.
- Method: stovetop roux + oven bake (400°F, 20 min)
- Type: baked mac and cheese with crushed Cheez-It topping

## Ingredients
- 1 lb large elbow macaroni (or cavatappi)
- 1/2 stick (4 tbsp) unsalted butter (plus extra for the pasta)
- 1/4 cup grated onion
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed (or 1 tsp garlic powder)
- 1 tsp mustard powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce ("washer sister sauce")
- 4 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 3 cups whole milk (or half-and-half)
- 16 oz (1 lb) white American cheese (shredded or freshly grated from the deli counter)
- 8 oz (1/2 lb) medium cheddar cheese, freshly grated
- 8 oz (1/2 lb) sharp cheddar cheese, freshly grated
- Topping: Crushed Cheez-Its and a sprinkle of cheddar jack cheese

## Instructions
1. Boil the Pasta: Cook the macaroni in boiling salted water for about 6 to 7 minutes until al dente. Drain it and fold in a little butter while it is still hot.
2. Make the Base: In a large pot or saucepan, melt the half-stick of butter over medium heat. Add the grated onion, garlic, mustard powder, smoked paprika, cayenne, black pepper, and Worcestershire sauce. Stir well.
3. Build the Roux: Sprinkle in the 4 tablespoons of flour. Stir constantly for about 1 minute to cook out the raw flour taste.
4. Add Milk: Slowly whisk in the whole milk. Keep whisking until the mixture is smooth and starts to thicken slightly.
5. Melt the Cheese: Turn the heat down or off the direct flame, then gradually add the white American cheese, medium cheddar, and sharp cheddar. Stir until completely melted, smooth, and gooey.
6. Combine: Fold the cooked pasta directly into the cheese sauce until every noodle is completely coated.
7. Bake: Transfer the mixture to a greased baking dish. Top with a generous layer of crushed Cheez-Its and cheddar jack cheese. Bake at 400°F for 20 minutes until bubbly and golden on top.
