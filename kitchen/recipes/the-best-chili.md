# The Best Chili Recipe (stovetop)

- Source: Holly Nilsson, Spend With Pennies
  (spendwithpennies.com/the-best-chili-recipe/)
- Servings: 8 (1 1/2 cups each) / Prep: 20 min / Cook: 45 min / Total: 1 hr 5 min
- Course: Main Course / Cuisine: American, Tex Mex
- Method: large pot, stovetop
- See also: `bottom-of-the-barrel-chili.md` (the user's other chili, crock pot)

## Ingredients
- 2 pounds lean ground beef
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 jalapeño, seeded and finely diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2½ tablespoons chili powder, divided, or to taste
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 green bell pepper, seeded and diced
- 1 (14.5 ounce) can crushed tomatoes
- 1 (19 ounce) can red kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 (14.5 ounce) can diced tomatoes with juices
- 1½ cups beef broth
- 1 cup beer
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar (optional)
- salt and black pepper, to taste

## Instructions
1. Combine ground beef and 1 ½ tablespoons chili powder.
2. In a large pot, brown ground beef, onion, jalapeño, and garlic. Drain any fat.
3. Add in remaining ingredients and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer uncovered for 45 to 60 minutes or until chili has reached desired thickness.
4. Top with cheddar cheese, green onions, cilantro or other favorite toppings.

## Notes
- Beer can be replaced with extra broth. Author uses a light beer like Budweiser;
  a darker beer also works.
- Any ground meat will work (chicken, turkey).
- Optional toppings: sour cream, red or green onion, cheese, jalapeños,
  cilantro, avocado and lime wedges, tortilla chips.
- Serve with cornbread, buttered toast, biscuits or dinner rolls, or over rice.
- To thicken: simmer uncovered longer; or sprinkle in cornmeal or add a
  cornstarch/flour slurry.
- Beans: pinto or black beans work too; chili beans add flavor (don't rinse those).
- More heat: leave jalapeño seeds in, add hot sauce or chili flakes.
- Tex-mex version: add a packet of taco seasoning.
- Freezes well; defrost overnight in the fridge. Tastes even better on day 2.
- Reader tips: some add more chili powder (about 2 tbsp extra) if it tastes
  light; some cut the broth back; for a crowd, 3 lbs beef plus extra beans.
