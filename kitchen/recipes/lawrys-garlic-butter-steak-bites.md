# Lawry's Garlic Butter Steak Bites

- Source: Lawry's (mccormick.com/blogs/lawrys-recipes/garlic-steak-bites). Text
  below came from a web-search summary the user pasted, not copied from the
  page, so amounts may be approximate.
- Method: skillet or cast-iron pan

## Ingredients
- 1 pound (16 ounces) ribeye steak, cut into 1/2-inch cubes
- 3 large Yukon gold potatoes, peeled and cut into 1/2-inch cubes
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2 tablespoons Lawry's® Coarse Ground With Parsley Garlic Salt
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 3 cloves minced garlic
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh rosemary
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh thyme

## Instructions
1. Prep the steak and potatoes: Wash and pat dry the ribeye steak, then cut it into 1/2-inch cubes. Peel and cube the Yukon gold potatoes.
2. Sear the steak: Heat the olive oil in a skillet or cast-iron pan over medium-high heat. Add the steak cubes in a single layer (cooking in batches if needed to avoid crowding) and season with Lawry's Garlic Salt. Sear undisturbed for about 2 minutes, then flip and cook for another 2 minutes until nicely browned or around 127°F internal temperature. Remove the steak to a plate.
3. Cook the potatoes: Add the cubed Yukon gold potatoes to the same hot skillet. Season with Lawry's seasoned salt and cook until soft and tender.
4. Make the garlic butter sauce: Lower the heat to medium-low. Add the unsalted butter, minced garlic, fresh rosemary, and fresh thyme to the pan. Stir almost constantly for 1 to 2 minutes until the garlic is fragrant and cooked, taking care not to let it burn.
5. Combine and serve: Return the steak bites (along with any resting juices from the plate) and the potatoes to the skillet. Toss quickly to coat in the garlic butter sauce, then remove from heat and serve immediately.

## Notes
- Step 3 says Lawry's "seasoned salt" while the ingredient list names the
  Coarse Ground With Parsley Garlic Salt. Likely the same seasoning used twice.
