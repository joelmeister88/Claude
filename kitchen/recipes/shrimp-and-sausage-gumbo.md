# Shrimp & Sausage Gumbo

- Source: Rian Handler / Delish Test Kitchen
  (delish.com/cooking/recipe-ideas/a54681/easy-seafood-gumbo-recipe/), updated Oct 8, 2025
- Yields: 4 servings / Prep: 20 mins / Total: 1 hr 20 mins / 633 cal per serving
- Method: deep skillet, stovetop
- Description: Creole-leaning gumbo built on a butter-and-flour roux and the
  "holy trinity" (onion, celery, bell pepper), with andouille, fire-roasted
  tomatoes and shrimp

## Ingredients
- 4 Tbsp. unsalted butter
- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 small yellow onion, chopped
- 1 medium green bell pepper, seeds and ribs removed, chopped
- 2 stalks celery, finely sliced
- 12 oz. andouille sausage, sliced into 1/2" rounds
- 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 1 Tbsp. Cajun seasoning (without salt)
- Kosher salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 (15-oz.) can fire-roasted diced tomatoes
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 lb. medium shrimp, peeled, deveined
- 3 scallions, sliced
- Cooked white rice, for serving

## Directions
1. In a large, deep skillet over medium-low heat, melt butter, then add flour. Cook, stirring constantly, until roux is dark caramel colored, 12 to 15 minutes.
2. Add onions, peppers, and celery and cook, stirring, until softened, about 8 minutes. Stir in sausage, garlic, and Cajun seasoning; season with salt and pepper. Stir in broth, tomatoes, and bay leaf and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to low and simmer, stirring occasionally, until thickened, about 1 hour.
3. In the last 6 minutes of cooking, add shrimp. Once shrimp is pink and cooked through, taste and adjust seasonings. Stir in scallions, reserving some for serving.
4. Divide rice among bowls. Spoon gumbo over. Top with reserved scallions.

## Notes from the article
- Don't walk away from the roux: stir almost constantly, it burns easily. If
  it burns, start over.
- Andouille matters. If you can't find it: ground pork + Cajun spices + about
  1/2 tsp liquid smoke.
- Use salt-free Cajun seasoning and low-sodium broth to control the salt.
- Shrimp shells add flavor if left on (optional). Shrimp are done as soon as
  they turn opaque/pink.
- Storage: refrigerate up to 2 days (it has seafood); freeze up to 6 months.
  Store cooked rice separately (fridge 2 days, freezer 1 month).
- Reader tips: some add okra with the shrimp; some boil the shrimp shells in a
  cup of water and use it as part of the broth; some find 4 cups of broth gives
  a thick stew and prefer more broth for a soupier gumbo.
- Sides suggested: fried okra, collard greens, cheese grits, cornbread.
