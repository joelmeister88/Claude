# Oven-Baked Mississippi Chicken

- Source: web recipe (plainchicken.com and similar). Text below came from a
  web-search summary the user pasted, not copied from a single page, so amounts
  may be approximate.
- Method: oven, 9x13-inch baking dish (about 30 minutes)
- Serve: over rice, noodles, or on rolls

## Ingredients
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (or thighs)
- 1 packet dry ranch seasoning mix
- 1 packet dry au jus gravy mix
- 1/4 to 1/3 cup sliced pepperoncini peppers plus 2–3 tablespoons of the juice
- 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, sliced into pats

## Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F and lightly grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
2. Place the chicken breasts in a single layer in the baking dish.
3. Sprinkle the dry ranch seasoning mix and the au jus mix evenly over the chicken.
4. Arrange the sliced pepperoncini peppers on top of the chicken and drizzle with the pepperoncini juice.
5. Place the butter pats evenly over the chicken and peppers.
6. Bake uncovered for 30 minutes, or until an internal meat thermometer reads 165°F.
7. Shred the chicken right in the pan so it soaks up the buttery juices, then serve over rice, noodles, or on rolls.
