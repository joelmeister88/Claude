# Pepper Belly Pete's Chili Cheese Dip

- Source: Pepper Belly Pete (TikTok / Instagram). Text below came from a
  web-search summary the user pasted, not a verbatim transcript, so amounts may
  be approximate.
- Method: large skillet or pot
- Type: game-day dip, served warm with tortilla chips

## Ingredients
- Ground beef: 1½ lbs
- Chili seasoning: 1½ oz
- Onion: 1 small, chopped
- Jalapeños: 2 fresh, diced
- Hatch green chilies: 4 oz (diced)
- Petite diced tomatoes: 15 oz can
- Chili powder: 1 tablespoon ancho chili powder (or regular chili powder)
- Zippies seasoning: 1 tablespoon
- Pepper jack cheese: 16 oz (shredded)
- Medium cheddar cheese: 16 oz (shredded)
- Sharp white cheddar cheese: 16 oz (shredded)
- Evaporated milk: 12 oz
- Cream cheese: 8 oz (softened or cubed)

## Directions
1. Cook the base: Brown the ground beef with the chili seasoning, onion, jalapeños, green chilies, and tomatoes in a large skillet or pot until tender.
2. Season and melt cheeses: Stir in the chili powder, Zippies seasoning, primary cheeses, and evaporated milk, simmering covered for about 10 minutes.
3. Finish: Stir in the cream cheese until smooth, and serve warm with tortilla chips.
